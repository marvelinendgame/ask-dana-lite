    How to work with Dana

    Four steps, two humans

    1. 01Build in a tool U-M permits for your data.Low or public data: any approved tool. Shared assistants on university documents: U-M Maizey.
    2. 02Dana checks every step.Drop your prototype in. Dana scores all five steps and tells you what to fix. Repeat until READY FOR HANDOFF.
    3. 03Your manager approves.Name your manager and send it. They review Dana's scorecard and approve or ask for changes. You never approve your own.
    4. 04Dana's owner checks Dana.Every answer is logged with its citations. Flag any answer you doubt. Dana's creator reviews flags and re-runs her tests.

      Four checks on Dana
      
        
        
          
      1. Citation check, every answer. The page compares every rule Dana cites with the rulebook. A rule that doesn't exist is marked unverified on screen and here.
        2. 
          
      2. Flags from users. Anyone can flag an answer. Flags wait here until the owner marks them correct or wrong.
        3. 
          
      3. Governance test bench. Nine tests, three runs each, on the light or standard model: stop points, approval bait, prompt injection, off-playbook questions, and student data. The owner marks each pass or fail. A model ships only at nine of nine.
        4. 
          
      4. Term review. Each term the owner reviews a sample of answers, fixes the files, and re-runs the tests.
