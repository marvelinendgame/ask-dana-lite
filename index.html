The same five steps and the same safeguards, sized for two people. One builds, one approves, and you swap.

Why

One path through U-M's guidance

U-M's AI guidance is strong, and it lives in many places: the SPGs, the Sensitive Data Guide, ITS AI Services, and the 2026 Guiding Principles. Ask Dana brings it into one path a new AI lead can follow. Ask Dana Lite keeps that path and drops the overhead a two-person team doesn't need.

The path

Five steps

  1. 01Frame the idea.Name the owner, the problem, and the risk.
  2. 02Check data and tools.Match the data to a tool U-M permits for it.
  3. 03Build and test.One task, cited sources, and tests written first.
  4. 04Keep a human in charge.A named reviewer who can switch it off.
  5. 05Hand off.Dana's scorecard goes to the person who signs.

The rules

Twelve must-haves

  1. Name the owner before building R0.1
  2. Decide if AI belongs here at all R0.3
  3. Flag sensitive uses: grades, admissions, hiring, health, discipline, money R0.5
  4. Check the data level in the Sensitive Data Guide R1.1
  5. Use only a tool the guide permits for that data R1.2
  6. No personal data without the data steward's approval R1.3
  7. Tell users they're talking to AI R2.4
  8. Build it accessibly R2.5
  9. Write tests first, including must-refuse questions R3.1
  10. A named human reviews outputs that affect people R4.1
  11. The builder never signs their own handoff R5.2
  12. Set a review date R5.4

The other 21 rules come back if the team grows or a prototype touches sensitive data.
